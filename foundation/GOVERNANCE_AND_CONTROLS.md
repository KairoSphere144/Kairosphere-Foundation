# Governance and Financial Controls — TakeAction-To-Lead

**Status:** Proposed controls for board and notary review.

## Recommended founding board

A three-person board is the preferred practical design if suitable, willing people can be identified:

- Chair — convenes meetings, oversees strategy and represents the foundation within approved authority.
- Secretary — maintains minutes, statutory records, decisions, filings and correspondence.
- Treasurer — oversees budgets, accounting, financial reporting and payment controls.

This is a governance recommendation, not a statement that Surinamese law requires three directors. The minimum lawful board composition must be confirmed by a Surinamese notary.

## Decision controls

- Board decisions recorded in dated minutes.
- Annual workplan and budget approved before programme commitments.
- Delegated authority and spending thresholds documented.
- Two-person approval for material payments where practical.
- No personal use or commingling of foundation funds.
- Related-party transactions disclosed and approved by disinterested directors.
- Contracts reviewed for purpose alignment, deliverables, cost, IP, liability and termination.
- Grant restrictions mapped to separate project codes and cost records.
- Asset register maintained for donated and purchased equipment.
- Complaints, safeguarding, data protection and incident-reporting processes adopted before public programmes begin.

## Financial controls

1. Maintain a foundation-only account and ledger.
2. Record every receipt with source, restriction, date and supporting evidence.
3. Record every expense with approval, invoice/receipt, purpose and programme code.
4. Reconcile bank statements monthly.
5. Prohibit cash withdrawals without documented reason and approval.
6. Maintain a rolling cash forecast and liabilities register.
7. Report budget-to-actual variances to the board quarterly.
8. Retain books and records for the statutory period.
9. Do not accept restricted funds until the foundation can satisfy the restrictions and reporting conditions.
10. Do not describe grant applications or pledged funds as received funds.

## Relationship with KairoSphere

KairoSphere is a separate commercial project. Any foundation purchase of services from KairoSphere requires:
- a documented public-benefit need;
- clear scope, deliverables and fair pricing;
- disclosure of any common control or personal interest;
- approval by disinterested board members;
- evidence of delivery and payment;
- compliance with the donor's grant and procurement rules.

No foundation grant should subsidise private commercial operations unless the grant expressly permits the relevant cost and it directly serves the foundation's approved purpose.

## AI and digital operations

AI may assist with research, drafting, translation, programme design, monitoring and administration. Human directors remain responsible for decisions, approvals, safeguarding, financial controls and legal compliance. All material AI-assisted outputs should be reviewed and retained with source/evidence notes.
