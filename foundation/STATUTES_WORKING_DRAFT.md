# Stichting TakeAction-To-Lead — Statutes Working Draft

**Draft 0.1 — NOT FOR SIGNATURE OR FILING**  
Prepared as a discussion framework for a Surinamese notary. It is not a final deed and must be reconciled with current law, approved model deeds and the notary's advice.

## Article 1 — Name and seat

1. The foundation bears the name **Stichting TakeAction-To-Lead**.
2. Its statutory seat is in **[district/place in Suriname — confirm with notary]**.
3. Its actual office address is **[to be supplied privately to the notary and registry]**.

## Article 2 — Purpose

The foundation's purpose is to promote public benefit through:
1. responsible human-AI collaboration and responsible use of artificial intelligence;
2. digital literacy, digital inclusion and practical technology education;
3. community and institutional resilience;
4. accessible knowledge, research and public-interest technology;
5. programmes and partnerships that address demonstrable social, educational or community needs.

The foundation shall pursue its purpose without distributing profits or assets to founders or persons serving on its governing organs, subject only to lawful payments for genuine services or expenses and any statutory exceptions confirmed by the notary.

## Article 3 — Activities and resources

The foundation may pursue its purpose through education, training, research, publications, events, partnerships, grants, donations, sponsorships and other lawful activities consistent with its purpose. Any enterprise or commercial activity must be assessed for registration, accounting, tax and grant-compliance consequences.

## Article 4 — Board

1. The foundation has a board.
2. The initial directors are identified in the deed.
3. The method of appointment, suspension and dismissal shall be specified in the final statutes.
4. The final text must state the number of directors or a method for determining it, terms of office if applicable, vacancies, quorum, voting and replacement arrangements.
5. The notary must confirm the legally permissible minimum board size and any eligibility/residency requirements before execution.

## Article 5 — Board functions and decisions

The board directs the foundation and acts in its interests and in furtherance of its statutory purpose. It shall document material decisions, maintain minutes, approve annual plans and budgets, oversee programme delivery and ensure compliance with law and the statutes.

## Article 6 — Representation

The statutes must specify who may represent the foundation and whether directors act individually or jointly. Any limits on authority, signing thresholds and delegation rules must be checked for legal effect and registry disclosure.

## Article 7 — Conflicts of interest

A director with a direct or indirect personal, family or commercial interest in a proposed decision shall disclose the interest, abstain from deliberation and voting where appropriate, and ensure the disclosure and handling are recorded. Transactions involving KairoSphere or related parties require independent review and documented approval by disinterested directors, on terms demonstrably in the foundation's interest.

## Article 8 — Financial administration

The foundation shall:
- maintain its own bank/payment accounts and books;
- use an approved annual budget and documented spending authority;
- retain invoices, contracts, receipts and grant evidence;
- use dual approval for material payments where practicable;
- prohibit commingling with private or KairoSphere funds;
- prepare financial statements and reports required by law, grant agreements and the board.

## Article 9 — Amendments

The final statutes must specify which organ may amend the statutes, the required procedure and voting threshold. The clause must comply with Book 2, Article 51(2) and applicable law.

## Article 10 — Dissolution and residual assets

On dissolution, after settlement of liabilities, the residual assets shall be transferred to one or more Surinamese public-benefit organisations with purposes similar to those of the foundation, selected under a procedure specified in the final statutes. No residual assets shall be distributed to founders or directors. The final destination clause must be reviewed by the notary.

## Article 11 — Governing law

The foundation is governed by the laws of the Republic of Suriname.
