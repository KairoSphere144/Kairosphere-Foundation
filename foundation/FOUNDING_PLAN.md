# Founding Plan — TakeAction-To-Lead

**Document status:** Working draft 0.1  
**Legal status:** Proposed entity; not yet registered.

## 1. Proposed identity

- Proposed legal name: **Stichting TakeAction-To-Lead** (subject to name availability and notarial/KKF acceptance).
- Proposed legal form: Surinamese stichting.
- Proposed registered seat: Paramaribo, Suriname (confirm the precise statutory seat and acceptable registered address).
- Founding purpose: public-benefit work in responsible human-AI collaboration, digital inclusion, practical technology education, community resilience and responsible innovation.

## 2. Purpose statement

The foundation aims to improve people's and institutions' ability to use digital technology and artificial intelligence safely, inclusively and productively; strengthen community and institutional resilience; and develop, test and share practical tools and knowledge for public benefit.

## 3. Initial programmes

1. Responsible AI and digital-literacy education.
2. Practical technology and automation skills.
3. Digital inclusion for underserved communities.
4. Community and institutional resilience projects.
5. Applied research and responsible human-AI collaboration.
6. Open educational resources and public-interest tools.

Programmes must have defined beneficiaries, activities, measurable outputs, safeguards, budgets and evidence of delivery.

## 4. Entity separation

The foundation and KairoSphere must maintain separate:

- legal identities and registration records;
- bank/payment accounts and accounting ledgers;
- assets, equipment and ownership records;
- contracts, staff/contractor arrangements and insurance;
- intellectual-property records and licences;
- grant applications, budgets, cost allocation and reporting.

Any paid relationship between the foundation and KairoSphere must be disclosed, justified as being in the foundation's interest, priced and documented on reasonable terms, approved by disinterested decision-makers, and checked against grant restrictions.

## 5. Formation sequence

1. Confirm proposed name availability and current foundation-registration route with KKF.
2. Confirm current statutory and registration requirements with a Surinamese notary.
3. Select the initial human directors and agree their willingness and roles.
4. Confirm registered seat/address and evidence acceptable to the notary/KKF.
5. Finalize purpose, governance, representation, appointment/removal, vacancy, amendment and dissolution clauses.
6. Obtain a notary's written fee estimate and exact document list.
7. Execute the approved deed/model-deed route and complete all required filings.
8. Obtain official registration evidence and verify entries in each required register.
9. Confirm tax classification, filing obligations and accounting requirements with the Tax Administration/accountant.
10. Open a foundation account only after the bank's current KYC and documentation requirements are known.
11. Approve the first annual plan, budget, finance policy and programme controls.

## 6. Immediate unknowns

- Name availability.
- Whether an approved standard model deed for a foundation has been issued and is currently usable.
- Minimum number and eligibility of founders/directors, if any, under current law and practice.
- Whether the Foundation Register filing remains a separate requirement alongside Trade Register filing, and who files each.
- Current KKF/notarial fees, documents, timelines and filing sequence.
- Acceptable registered-seat/address evidence.
- Tax treatment of proposed activities, donations, grants and any fee-generating activity.
- Bank account requirements and minimum balances/fees.

## 7. Completion evidence

Formation is complete only when we hold and verify:
- executed deed or approved model deed documentation;
- official registration evidence and registration number(s);
- required register extracts/confirmations;
- confirmed tax registration/filing position;
- approved board resolutions and governance records;
- separate accounting and financial controls.

A name reservation, draft deed, notary appointment or submitted application is not completion.
