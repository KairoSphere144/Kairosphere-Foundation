# TakeAction-To-Lead — Foundation Formation Workspace

**Status:** Formation preparation only. The foundation is not represented as registered or operational.

This directory holds the working formation package for a proposed Surinamese *stichting* named **TakeAction-To-Lead**. It is deliberately separate from KairoSphere's commercial operations.

- [Founding plan](FOUNDING_PLAN.md)

- [Registration and verification checklist](REGISTRATION_CHECKLIST.md)
- [Governance and financial controls](GOVERNANCE_AND_CONTROLS.md)

## Evidence rules

Every item must be labelled as one of:

- **VERIFIED** — supported by an official source or issued document.
- **REPORTED** — supplied by a participant but not independently checked.
- **PENDING** — requires confirmation from a notary, KKF, tax authority or other competent institution.
- **NOT STARTED** — no action yet.

Do not describe a draft, enquiry, application, appointment or verbal indication as registration, approval, funding or completion.

## Legal baseline

The principal statutory starting point is Book 2, Title 2, Articles 50–58 of Suriname's Civil Code (S.B. 2024 no. 164), in force from 1 May 2025. The official government announcement confirms commencement. The final deed and filing sequence must be checked against current implementing legislation and registry practice.

- [Civil Code, S.B. 2024 no. 164 (PDF)](https://www.sris.sr/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/S.B-2024-no.-164-Burgerlijk-Wetboek.pdf)
- [Government commencement announcement](https://gov.sr/inwerkingtreding-nieuw-burgerlijk-wetboek-vindt-voortgang-op-1-mei-2025/)
- [Government business-permit process](https://gov.sr/ministeries/ministerie-van-economische-zaken-ondernemerschap-technologische-innovatie/bedrijfsvergunning/)
